Snip Studio privacy policy

Last updated: 6 October 2026

Snip Studio is a Chrome extension for screenshots, GIFs, and screen recordings. This page describes what happens to data when you use it.

Captures stay on your device

Screenshots, GIF frames, and videos are created in the browser and stored in local extension storage (IndexedDB). We do not operate a server that receives your captures. Uninstalling the extension removes that local data.

What we do not collect

  • Browsing history
  • The contents of pages you capture, except as pixels stored locally for the capture you requested
  • Advertising identifiers
  • Analytics SDKs from Snip Studio

Permissions

The extension can access the current tab and websites so it can draw a region picker, record a GIF overlay, scroll for full-page shots, and save the image or video you asked for. It does not inject ads.

Payments (optional Pro)

If you buy Pro ($4.99 one-time), checkout and license status are handled by ExtensionPay. Their privacy policy applies to payment. Snip Studio only stores whether this browser is licensed.

Language setting

Your UI language choice is saved in chrome.storage on this device.

Contact

Questions: use the support email listed on the Chrome Web Store item, or the repository linked from the listing.
